My Story

I'm Victory Lord Martins, a self-taught developer from Nigeria with over 4 years of experience building tools for the community. I started coding at 18, and since then, I've built everything from Telegram bots to AI-powered tools.

My mission is simple: make powerful technology accessible to everyone. I believe that high-quality tools shouldn't be locked behind paywalls or complex systems. That's why I built VL Mail.

VL Mail is my vision of a premium, developer-friendly email platform that gives you freedom, security, and the features you actually need.
